## NOT presented as verified AccessByteSolutions business facts

The reference message describes TechMore IT Solutions' experience, capacity and customers. This does **not** establish that AccessByteSolutions has the same capabilities. The following must not be publicly attributed to AccessByteSolutions without written evidence:

1. 2,000 pages per day or 80,000+ pages per month production throughput.
2. Past work for Aetna, Anthem, Molina, WellCare, CareSource, Coordinated Care or Blue Cross.
3. Any ISO, PDF/UA, ADA or other formal company *certification* without a verifiable certificate.
4. Any legal guarantee of 100% compliance.
5. A precise office address or Google Maps location.
6. Availability of specific multilingual specialists (English, Spanish, Russian etc.) on every project.

The website includes standards and languages as *scope topics*, not unconditional guarantees. If proof is provided, specific factual claims can be approved and added to the website later.

## Frontend limitations

This package contains HTML/CSS/JavaScript only. A true upload, automated document remediation, backend storage, CRM, email sending, live project status, verifiable testimonials and real certification listings require separate systems.

The project enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message that the visitor must manually send. A selected file stays on the visitor's device and is not automatically attached to WhatsApp.
