# Feature and coverage map

## Service categories
- PDF remediation, PDF tagging/reading order, tables, alt text, links, bookmarks, fillable forms and OCR.
- Word, Excel, PowerPoint and multilingual document accessibility.
- Website audit, manual and automated testing, WCAG, keyboard, screen-reader, ARIA, form and contrast support.
- Compliance: WCAG 2.1/2.2, PDF/UA, Section 508, ADA, AODA, ACA, EAA/EN 301 549, RGAA and BITV.
- VPAT/ACR reporting and accessibility consulting.
- White-label translation/localization and agency delivery.

## Reference coverage and limits
Reference feature categories reviewed from techmoreitsolutions.com and aligotech.com on 28 Oct 2026. The site implements **public-facing frontend patterns and analogous service coverage**, not copies of proprietary content, certifications, invented portfolios, business claims, visitor data or hidden backends. In particular, certificates, job applications, chat systems, file processors, payment systems and live audit engines are not implemented, as they require legitimate business data or server integrations.
